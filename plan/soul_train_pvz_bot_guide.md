# Soul Train (7 Gate Robo) — PvZ Bot Coding Guide
*Extracted from: "StarCraft 2 - PvZ - Le NOUVEAU SOUL TRAIN Avec Rom!" (LotV)*
*Presented by Rom, GM Protoss player*

---

## What Is This Build

The Soul Train is a 2-base Protoss all-in targeting Zerg's third base before Mutalisks or Infestors come online. It uses 3 Immortals, a high Sentry count, and Force Fields to surgically cut the Zerg army. It hits before 6 minutes. The Warp Prism is used to save injured key units (Immortals, Sentries) and deliver reinforcements mid-fight.

This is not cheese. The Zerg can see it coming and still lose if your execution is clean.

---

## Phase 1: Opening and Economy Setup

### Probe Production Priority
- After the first Pylon finishes, queue a Probe IMMEDIATELY and chrono it.
- The Probe queued right after the Pylon is the one that goes to take gas at the natural (B2). Do not delay this — a half-second delay on gas is significant. A half-second delay on the Gateway is not.
- Probe count target: stop at ~45 total. Optimal saturation is 16 on minerals per base and 3 in each gas (4 Assimilators total).

### Probe Allocation at Natural (B2)
- Send probes to the natural with this split: 2 on gas, 16 on minerals.
- This build is mineral-limited, not gas-limited. The Robo Facility will bottleneck on minerals first. Reflect this in your probe assignments.

### Chrono Boost Rules
- When the Nexus at B2 finishes: chrono B2 only. Do not chrono the main Nexus.
- Once the Robo Facility and Warp Gate research are both started: you can then chrono both Nexuses. This gives you an economy buffer slightly later in the game.
- Chrono the Robo Facility continuously once Immortal production begins.
- Do NOT chrono the Warp Prism.

## Phase 2: Build Order

### Structure Sequence (Critical Ordering)
1. First Gateway
2. Cybernetics Core
3. **Adept** (first unit from the Gateway)
4. **Robo Facility** — must be placed BEFORE the additional Gateways
5. Warp Gate research begins at the Cybernetics Core
6. 4 more Gateways (total 5 at this point, plus 2 more added later = 7)

**The Robo Facility must go down before Warp Gate finishes.** This is intentional — placing Robo after the Adept but before the extra Gates delays the tell that this is not a Stargate opener. A good Zerg scout sees the Cybernetics Core light up and reads "Stargate." Delaying the Robo placement preserves that ambiguity.

### Unit Production Sequence
1. Adept (Gateway 1 — used for scouting)
2. Sentry
3. Sentry
4. Begin Immortal production (Robo, chrono continuously)
5. Continue producing Sentries from all Gates until 3 Immortals finish
6. After 3rd Immortal finishes: queue Warp Prism, then Observer

### Gate Warp-in Composition
Your gates are desynchronized by design. Here is what happens during the march:
- First group of gates fires: ~3 Sentries
- Second group fires: ~4 units
- Third group fires: ~3 units
- Total at the fight: approximately 10 Zealots, 6 Sentries, 3 Immortals

Warp in Zealots when your Zergling count is high. Warp in Stalkers when facing heavy Roaches.

### Home Defense During the Push
- Leave your Adept inside your base (it's useless in the frontal fight but useful for base defense).
- Leave your Stalker near the wall.
- Place a Battery/Shield Battery at the wall to guard against run-bys.

## Phase 3: The Push

### Timing
- Exit your base between 5:12 and 5:35. Before 5:35 is the target window; perfect execution gets you to 5:12.
- The goal: arrive at the Zerg's third base BEFORE 6 minutes.

### Army Composition on Push
- 3 Immortals
- ~6 Sentries
- ~10 Zealots
- Warp Prism (for evacuation and warp-in reinforcements)
- Observer (anti-burrow)

### Proxy Pylon
Send a Probe ahead to build proxy Pylons as close to the Zerg's third base as possible. Warping in from Pylons lets the Warp Prism focus entirely on protecting the Immortals. The Warp Prism is not a reinforcement bus — it is a life-support system for your key units.

### Movement Mindset
Do not hesitate. Move straight at the Zerg. Do not wait to collect more units. This timing is your window. If you stall, Ravagers come online and Force Fields stop working.

---

## Phase 4: The Fight

### Force Field Placement
This is the entire build. Force Fields go in a semi-circle arc in the MIDDLE of the Zerg army. The goal is to split it in half — trap half while you kill it, then clean up the second half.

- Use Force Fields defensively around your OWN army's flanks when the Zerg pushes forward.
- Do NOT Force Field in front of the Ravagers — Force Field the SIDES to stop Zerglings from flanking.

### Engagement Rules
- Zealots are the front line, always forward, never retreat.
- Immortals stay in the middle/back — they deal massive bonus damage to armored units.
- Sentries behind Zealots, roughly level with the Immortals.
- Stalkers and Trackers warp in AFTER the main fight starts — roughly half-half mix, primarily to range-attack and stop Zerglings draining your army from behind.

### Warp Prism Micro
- If an Immortal drops below half health: pick it up, move it to safety, unload it immediately so it gets back into the fight.
- If Sentries are being surrounded: extract them the same way.
- Do NOT let the Warp Prism get hit by Queens or Hydralisks — it must survive.

### Ravager Bile Dodge
Dodge Ravager Corrosive Biles. This is non-negotiable. Biles chunking an Immortal early collapses the push.

### Three Scenarios at the Fight

**Scenario 1: Roach + Ravager**
- Force Field arc in the center of the Zerg army.
- Dodge Biles.
- Focus Roaches with Immortals.
- If no Zerglings: shift focus to Roaches purely.

**Scenario 2: Heavy unit count (Zerg outpaced your timing)**
- Switch to defensive Force Fields — surround your OWN army with them to buy time.
- Stop pushing. Accumulate units until you outnumber them.
- Force Fields at army flanks to prevent arcing attacks.
- Keep a reserve of ~10 Zealots as a fixed unit count minimum.

**Scenario 3: Ravager + Zergling flood (Zerg went gas-heavy)**
- This is the hardest scenario. Ravagers + Lings = potential surround.
- Force Field your FLANKS, not in front of the Ravagers.
- Do not let Zerglings make contact with your Sentries and Immortals.
- If they surround you: you lose your full army to 12 Zerglings. Force Field is the only answer.

## Phase 5: After the Push

### If You Broke the Third Base
You win. But here is what to do:
- Take your own third base.
- Take your 4th gas.
- Add tech: 2-3 Disruptors (Zerg stuck on Roaches = hard countered by Disruptors).
- Add a Forge if you lost Sentries and need to rebuild your army quality.
- Keep 4 Sentries in reserve for any defensive Force Field need.

### If the Push Was Stopped
You are in trouble. Here is the recovery:
- Rebuild Immortals and Sentries.
- Scout with Observer: Mutalisks incoming = Twilight Council + Blink research. Infestors incoming = Colossi.
- Take a third if you can. If you cannot, max out on 2 bases and go Colossus all-in.
- Do not go Brood Lord. You do not have the economy.

### If You Miss the Timing (But Still Have Units)
Do not give up. Save your Immortals with the Warp Prism. Try to trade well. Sentries are probably dead — accept it. The game is not over if you conserve your Immortals.

## Technical Requirements (For the Bot)

### Control Groups Needed
- 1 control group: Nexus (for chrono)
- 1 control group: Robo Facility (continuous Immortal production)
- 1 control group: Warp Prism (micro unit extraction)
- 1 control group: Army (main combat group)
- 1 optional: Sentries (isolated Force Field casting)

### Rapid Fire Requirement
Zealot warp-ins require rapid fire (repeated keypresses for each gate warp-in). Normal single-command warp-in across 8 gates costs multiple seconds of delay. Rapid fire warp-in is mandatory for correct timing.

### Probe Micro at Natural
If APM allows: micro B2 Probes manually onto the optimal mineral patches (closest minerals first) to maximize income. If not, a simple rally to minerals is acceptable. The bottleneck is minerals, so even small inefficiency here matters.

## Conditions Summary (When to Use This Build)

Use it when:
- Zerg is confirmed on 3 bases (Zealot scout confirms third base in production)
- Zerg gas confirms droning (no early all-in signals)
- Map has a short rush distance and natural chokes

Do NOT use it when:
- Zerg cut drones and went mass Roach early
- Banelings are confirmed (they destroy Sentry clusters)
- Map is large/open with wide ramps near the Zerg third
